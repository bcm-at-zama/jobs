### Next step suggestion

Build a generic Workday fetcher (`kind: "workday"`). Most Workday sites share
the same JSON API pattern:
  POST https://<tenant>.wd<N>.myworkdayjobs.com/wday/cxs/<tenant>/<board>/jobs
with a search payload. If we crack that, we unlock ~20 companies at once
(Amazon, Adobe, Salesforce, Intel, Qualcomm, IBM, Uber, Netflix, Palantir,
Tesla, Booking, PayPal, Cisco, Unity, VMware, Zoom, Oracle, Spotify).
