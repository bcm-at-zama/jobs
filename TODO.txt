================================================================
Workday usage — adding a new Workday-hosted company (3 minutes)
================================================================

1. Find the company's Workday URL. It looks like:
      https://<tenant>.wd1.myworkdayjobs.com/<BoardName>
   Some are wd3, wd5, etc. (the number is the Workday data-center).
   Look at what the "Careers" link redirects to.

2. Add to config.py SOURCES:
      {"name": "Adobe", "kind": "workday", "slug": "adobe",
       "queries": ["security", "cryptography", "CTO", "VP"],
       "board": "https://adobe.wd5.myworkdayjobs.com/external_experienced"},

3. Add to COMPANY_INFO with a short blurb + headcount + revenue.
4. Add to GROUP_OF ("Big Tech" or "Security Companies" etc.).
5. Run `python3 jobs.py --clear-cache list --skip-llm --only Adobe` to verify.
